# Roadmap (draft)

## Phase 0: Discovery
- User interviews with young mothers
- Competitor review (BabyCenter, Flo, Huckleberry, local Telegram channels)
- Finalize MVP scope

## Phase 1: Design
- Brand: name, logo, colors, type
- Wireframes: onboarding, home, trackers, weekly guide, vaccines
- Clickable prototype, test with 5 moms

## Phase 2: MVP build
- App shell, navigation, localization (uz-Latn)
- Onboarding + local profile
- Trackers (feed, sleep, diaper) + growth log
- Week-by-week content
- Vaccination calendar + reminders
- Closed beta on Android

## Phase 3: Launch
- Google Play release, then App Store
- Analytics and crash reporting
- Russian localization

## Phase 4: Grow
- Accounts and sync (backend)
- Community / Q&A
- Partner and family sharing
- Monetization experiments
