# 12 Moon keepsake research

How 14 apps mark a month, show a moon, ask for a few words, and turn it all into a card worth sending, and what oyjon's monthly moon can borrow. This is the text version of the "Moon keepsake research" section on the Moon keepsake page of the [oyjon-flow FigJam board](https://www.figma.com/board/ZyESbImvUcKhpd37KaCcBN/oyjon-flow). The screenshots live on the board only.

Researched 13 Oct 2026. Mobbin has no baby trackers, so Gaia, Huckleberry, Nara and Glow come from their sites and App Store pages. The other 10 are moon, recap, share and journal apps on Mobbin.

## 1. The flows

### Baby trackers not on Mobbin

**Gaia Baby.** Notes from [gaiababy.app](https://gaiababy.app/).

- No keepsakes or milestones of its own. Milestones only arrive through a Nara import.
- "No streaks. No daily goal." A tracker, not a memory book.

**Huckleberry.** Notes from the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/huckleberry-baby-tracker/id1169136078).

- Tracks milestones. No monthly card or keepsake.
- Sharing is about showing trends to the pediatrician.

**Nara Baby.** Notes from the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1444639029).

- Milestones by age, with notes and photos for moments like a first smile or first tooth.
- Photos can sit on feeding sessions too. No monthly card.

**Glow Baby.** Notes from the App Store listing.

- The listing doesn't describe a monthly keepsake.

None of the four turns a month into something to send. That gap is the moon keepsake.

### Flows on Mobbin

**[Stardust, home](https://mobbin.com/flows/2e98fee1-87cb-4fbd-b9a7-b4df7ea4310e).** 10 of 16 screens.

- Home is a ring of moon phases in cycle colours, with "Period in 23 days" in the middle.
- A strip of days with little moons runs under it. You can scroll back and forward.
- "Your vibe today" and "429,810 other Starlings are also in their follicular phase" feel personal and shared at once.
- "Share Stardust" sits at the bottom of a fact card.
- The closest app to our moon. Busy, though. Our moon should be one shape, not twenty.

**[Moonlitt, details and share](https://mobbin.com/flows/d4043e8a-7b8e-4249-acb0-96233acbeca9).** 6 screens with [Moon detail](https://mobbin.com/flows/8a1e63cf-51f8-4ac1-9c61-2a567661a3b0).

- One moon on a deep blue sky. A chip says "New Moon 1.8%".
- The details say "Full in 13 days, 21 hr" and "New in 29 days".
- A row of moon cards to swipe through days, each with one line about the phase.
- The share screen is just the moon in a card, its name, the date, and one Share button.

**[Weather, moon phase](https://mobbin.com/flows/5b296190-9468-4465-a2bc-96f497a8dd71).** 4 screens.

- A small moon tile on the weather page: illumination, moonrise, "Next Full Moon 2 days".
- Tap it for a big moon and a month calendar where every day has its own tiny moon.
- Plain "About Illumination" text explains what the percentage means.

**[Duolingo, year in review](https://mobbin.com/flows/8571a01f-da27-4bd6-a43b-e6b59ebadcd6).** 3 screens.

- "Share your progress" over a card with Duo, the year and four numbers.
- Swipe between two card designs.
- The share sheet has three buttons: Messages, Save image, More.

**[Beli, monthly recap](https://mobbin.com/flows/6520099e-59a7-44a2-8336-a80d9feb47ce).** 4 screens.

- A bright "Your September Recap" banner on the home feed starts it.
- Story-style pages with dots at the top, each a different card.
- A row of targets at the bottom: IG Story, IG Post, TikTok, Messages, Save.

**[Strava, month in sport](https://mobbin.com/flows/38aaf14f-cd60-4348-bc6c-4caddcb33ac8).** 3 screens.

- A dark card with the month's numbers and "STRAVA MAY 2026" running along the bottom.
- "Customize & share" and "Replay" under it.
- The share sheet shows two card styles side by side, then Message, Strava Post, Save, More.

**[Retro, creating a recap](https://mobbin.com/flows/793db4a9-d398-449e-9f5c-59792ba3aab1).** 9 screens.

- Pick Monthly, then a month. It builds a photo grid with "May" in big serif type.
- The share screen shows the card big, with Share and Save buttons under it.
- "Saved to camera roll" pops up as a short toast.

**[stoic., daily journal](https://mobbin.com/flows/8165f421-cd01-40f3-ae23-e1ad1398ba43).** 7 screens.

- One question a day on a white card, like "Make a list of 3 things...".
- A week of days on top gets a tick for each answered day.
- The quote journal offers "Empty Page" or "Random Prompt". You can always skip the question.

**[Liven, journal](https://mobbin.com/flows/e9484039-b881-42cf-8722-41877dbb7b3a).** 3 screens.

- "What's on your mind?" with a small "Suggest a question" chip above it.
- "Begin where you are. One small journal entry can change the day."
- Entries then show on a timeline with the time and a mood word.

**[Instagram, story to close friends](https://mobbin.com/flows/bc7ecec2-46a2-48e9-aa90-4a9f91c8aacd).** 4 of 8 screens.

- Where our card lands. Share opens Your story, Close Friends or Message.
- Close Friends is the private option a mum might use for a baby picture.

### Patterns

- **A reveal comes before the share.** Strava, Duolingo and Beli show the card full screen first. Sharing is the second step.
- **The card is big and the buttons are small.** Every share screen puts the picture first and a short row of targets under it.
- **Save image is always there.** Duolingo, Strava, Beli and Retro all offer it next to Share.
- **Adult recaps are numbers.** Strava, Duolingo and Beli brag with stats. For a baby the story is the age and the mum's own words. Stats would turn it into a score.
- **Two styles to swipe.** Duolingo and Strava let you pick between two card designs.
- **The app name sits small at the bottom.** Beli, Retro and Strava sign the card without shouting.
- **Moon apps show the whole cycle.** Stardust's ring, Weather's calendar of tiny moons, Moonlitt's "Full in 13 days".
- **Journals give a prompt and a way out.** stoic.'s "Random Prompt", Liven's "Suggest a question", and skip always works.
- **Recaps are announced quietly on home.** Beli's banner, not a pop-up.
- **A small toast confirms the save.** Retro's "Saved to camera roll".

## 3. Ideas for oyjon

v1 only. M1 and M2, with M3 Journey on Later. Each idea has a priority.

1. **M1 is one big moon (must).** It fills a little each day of the baby's month. Under it, "Oy 4 · 12-kun" and "3 oy 12 kun". One line says when it's full: "Toʻlin oyga 18 kun".
2. **The month-day is the birth date (must).** Born on the 7th, every 7th is a full moon. Born on the 31st, short months use their last day.
3. **One quiet signal (must).** A notification at 10:00, "4-oy toʻldi", only if she turned notifications on. Either way, a card on Today says the moon is full.
4. **One sentence before the card (must).** Oyim asks "What do you want to remember from month 4?" Three prompt chips help, like a first smile, a funny moment, how you felt. Skip always works.
5. **M2 is a memory, not a report (must).** The full moon, "4 oylik", the baby's name, the month's dates, her sentence, and a small oyjon at the bottom. No weights or feed counts.
6. **Made on the phone, story size (must).** 9:16, works offline, in her language and script.
7. **Two buttons (must).** "Saqlash" saves to the gallery with a short "Saved" toast. "Ulashish" opens the phone's share sheet, where Telegram and Instagram already are.
8. **The card waits (must).** Miss the day and M1 says "4-oy tayyor" until she opens it. Nothing expires and nothing nags.
9. **Edit the sentence later (must).** Fix a typo and save again.
10. **Hide the name (should).** One toggle on M2, for a public Instagram.
11. **Two card styles to swipe (should).** Day paper and night sky.
12. **A row of past moons on M1 (should).** Tap one to reopen its card. A light version of M3 Journey, which stays on Later.
13. **A photo on the card (later).** Needs photo storage and its own privacy check.
14. **Square and other sizes (later).**
15. **A printed year of moons (later).**
16. **A moving card or HD version (later).**

### Proposed v1 flow

Four taps from the notification to a sent card.

1. The month-day arrives. A 10:00 notification if she turned them on, and a card on Today either way (must)
2. M1, the moon fills and glows (must)
3. Written this month's sentence? If not, Oyim asks for one, and skip works (must)
4. M2 keepsake card (must)
5. Save to the phone, or Share to Telegram or Instagram (must)
6. Back on M1, the moon stays full until the next month starts (must)

## 4. Decisions

All picked by me on 13 Oct 2026 so the work could keep going. Each is open for the user to keep or change.

1. The card is story size, 9:16, in v1. My pick: yes.
2. No health numbers on the card, only age, name, dates and her sentence. My pick: yes.
3. No baby photo on the card in v1. My pick: later.
4. The notification comes at 10:00 and only if she turned notifications on. Today shows a card either way. My pick: yes.
5. The month-day is the birth date, or the month's last day when it's shorter. My pick: yes.
6. The card never expires. "4-oy tayyor" waits on M1. My pick: yes.
7. Save plus the phone's share sheet, no separate Telegram button. My pick: yes.
8. A toggle to hide the baby's name on the card. My pick: keep as should.
9. A row of past moons on M1 to reopen old cards. My pick: keep as should, with M3 staying on Later.
10. Two card styles to swipe. My pick: keep as should.

## 5. UX review

Done on 13 Oct 2026. I walked the month-day as a mum with notifications off, one who didn't open the app for six weeks, one who can barely type, and one who wants to post on public Instagram. The diagram on the [Moon keepsake page](https://www.figma.com/board/ZyESbImvUcKhpd37KaCcBN/oyjon-flow?node-id=12-886) now shows each fix as a numbered badge.

Four taps from the notification to a sent card.

### Revised flow

1. The month-day arrives. A 10:00 notification only if she turned them on, and it waits a day if A3 opened that day.
2. A Today card either way. It stays until she opens the moon or closes it.
3. M1, the moon fills and Oyim turns full. A tap skips the moment. Several waiting moons all stay ready.
4. No sentence yet? Oyim asks for one. Chips start it, 120 characters, skip works.
5. M2 keepsake card. Hide name also hides the dates. Moon 12 says "1 yosh".
6. Saqlash or Ulashish. Gallery access is asked right there. Offline, save works and the share waits in Telegram.
7. Back on M1, full until the next month-day.

### Issues and fixes

| # | Priority | What goes wrong | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | must | Two moons fill while she's away for six weeks | Every waiting moon stays ready: "4-oy va 5-oy tayyor" |
| 2 | must | A Today card that never leaves | Stays until she opens the moon or closes it. Closing keeps the card on M1 |
| 3 | should | A full-moon cheer on the day she called 103 | The notification waits a day if A3 opened that day |
| 4 | must | A blank text box is hard for a tired or low-literacy mum | Chips start the sentence. Skip works, and a card without one still looks finished |
| 5 | must | Long text breaks a 9:16 card, Cyrillic and Russian run longer | 120 characters, counter near the end |
| 6 | should | The month's dates give away the birth date on a public post | Hide name also hides the dates |
| 7 | must | Old Androids need storage access to save | Ask when she taps Saqlash. Ulashish works either way |
| 8 | should | Moon 12 is the first birthday | Card says "1 yosh", moons keep counting after |
| 9 | should | Fixing the birth date moves the moon | Saved cards stay as made, Settings warns first |
| 10 | should | Offline | The card is made on the phone. Save works, a Telegram share waits |
| 11 | should | The full-moon moment can drag | 2 to 3 seconds, a tap skips it, still with Reduce Motion |
