Board: Oyim · first sketch, node 41:2 on the 05b Brand archive page.

Personality: **Oyim**, a quiet older-sister moon who speaks rarely and smiles a lot (Shokhnur's pick, 2026-10-12). Rules are in [`../docs/spec/companion.md`](../docs/spec/companion.md).

- One glossy saffron circle with no limbs, lit like the Night app icon.
- Moods come from moon phases. A shadow crossing her face means thinking or sleepy; full with a glow ring means proud.
- States drawn so far: idle, saved, thinking, sleepy, proud, each on paper and at night, plus sizes 160, 64, 32 and 24.

Watch out: a round yellow face can read as a generic emoji. Her own details (the moon phase shadow, craters, a softer cream-saffron, a tiny crescent mark) should keep her distinct. She never gives advice and she's hidden in loss mode.

`companion.md` still lists cream and honey colours and the Grok avatar style. Both were written before the brand was locked; the saffron and indigo sketch replaces them once approved.
