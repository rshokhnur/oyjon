# Brand (draft)

**Name:** Oyjon ("dear mom" in Uzbek). Warm, familiar, personal.

**Voice:** like a kind older sister who is also well-informed. Calm, encouraging, never preachy or scary. Short sentences.

## Colors (placeholder)

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| background | `#FFF9F6` | App background, warm off-white |
| surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards |
| primary | `#D9776A` | Main actions, accents (soft terracotta) |
| secondary | `#7FA99B` | Secondary accents (sage) |
| text | `#2E2A28` | Body text |
| textMuted | `#7A706B` | Captions, hints |

Mirrored in code at `apps/mobile/src/theme.ts`.

## Type

System fonts for now. Pick a rounded, friendly typeface with full Uzbek Latin and Cyrillic support (e.g. Nunito) during the design phase.
