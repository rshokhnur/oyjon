# Step 4: wireframes

Page: [04 Wireframes](https://www.figma.com/design/bg1FIs5wZOY9m0wUWkTJTe/oyjon-app?node-id=3-5) in the design file.

Rebuilt on 13 Oct 2026 from the decided flows in `08` to `13` and the country rules in [`14-global-ready.md`](14-global-ready.md). The old 9 English screens are gone.

51 grey screens, 390 x 844, grouped by flow in the same order as the FigJam board. Plain greys, no brand colour yet. Titles are Source Serif 4 and the interface is Onest. All copy is uz-Latn with Uzbekistan as the default country. Each screen has its code, its state, a "why it's here" line and notes on what happens around it.
