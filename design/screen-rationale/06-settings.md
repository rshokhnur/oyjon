# Settings: where each screen comes from and why each part is there

Wireframes: the "6 Settings" row on [04 Wireframes](https://www.figma.com/design/bg1FIs5wZOY9m0wUWkTJTe/oyjon-app?node-id=3-5). References are the apps in [`../13-settings-research.md`](../13-settings-research.md).

Settings holds the few things she might change, plus the things the law and her trust need: consent, export and delete. None of it is ever paywalled.

## S1 Settings

**Based on:** Lifesum (groups, help near the bottom), Manus (each row shows its current value), Visible (export sits as a normal row, not hidden away).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| Close × | Settings is a sheet over Today, not a tab. |
| Baby card | The baby comes first, here too. Opens the profile. |
| Til, Mamlakat, Bildirishnomalar, Tungi rejim | The four preferences, each showing its value so she doesn't have to open it to check. |
| Hisob, Maxfiylik va maʼlumotlar | Backup status and her data rights, kept apart from preferences. |
| Oyimning soʻzlari switch | Turns off Oyim's text but keeps her. The companion rules promise this choice. |
| Yordam | Help and the emergency row. |

## S3 Baby profile

**Based on:** Visible's profile, kept to one baby.

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| Moon avatar | The same moon as Today, so it's clearly the same baby. |
| Ism, Tugʻilgan sana, Jinsi, vazn rows | Names come days after the birth, and dates get typed wrong. |
| Birth date warning sheet | The moon and charts move with the date. Saved cards stay as made. |

## S7 Language and script

**Based on:** Manus (each language in its own script), Hypelist (the title switches at once, so you see it worked).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| Four languages in their own names | She can find her language even if the app is in one she can't read. |
| Switches at once | No restart. |
| Undo toast in the old language | A wrong tap into a script she can't read is undone in one tap. |
| Line about Oyim and cards | Tells her answers follow the language, and saved cards don't change. |

## S8 Notifications

**Based on:** Stoic (a sample next to each reminder).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| "Blocked in phone settings" card | A switch that silently does nothing breaks trust. One button opens the right page. |
| Two switches with an example each | Only two notifications exist in v1, both off until she says yes. |

## S9 Account and backup

**Based on:** none directly. It answers the question every reference ignores: is my diary safe?

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| "Kundalik hali zaxiralanmagan" | Says plainly that a lost phone means a lost diary. |
| "Zaxiralash" | Goes to the sign-in step from onboarding. |

## S6 Privacy and data

**Based on:** Binance's Privacy Center (everything about your data on one page), Visible (export as a normal row).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| Health data switch | Switching it off explains that the diary will be deleted, then leads into Delete. |
| Question review switch | The optional consent from onboarding, changeable any time. |
| Maʼlumotlarni yuklab olish | One spreadsheet made on the phone, in her language. Not LinkedIn's "use a computer". |
| Maxfiylik siyosati | Required, and one tap away. |
| "Kundalikni oʻchirish" in its own group | Easy to find, hard to hit by accident. |

## S6 Delete diary

**Based on:** Formula 1 and Wanderlog (a grace period with a clear end), DailyArt (asks why, "Decline to answer"), Wolt (a counter of days left).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| "Nusxa saqlash" first | Most people regret losing the baby's first months. |
| Reason chips with "Aytmayman" | Teaches the team why mums leave, never forced. |
| What happens, in two lines | Phone data goes now, the server copy after 7 days, and signing in brings it back. |
| Oʻchirish, Bekor qilish | One confirm. Cancel is always next to it. |

## S10 Help and about

**Based on:** Lifesum ("Sources of recommendations" as its own row, version at the bottom).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| "103 ga qoʻngʻiroq" row on top | Emergency help without needing a red flag. The number comes from her country. |
| "Kartalarni kim tekshiradi" | Who stands behind Oyim's answers. |
| "Telegram orqali yozish" | A human to write to, where Uzbek mums already are. Email elsewhere. |
| Terms, privacy, version | Required, and for support to know which version she has. |

## Drop proposals

1. **The "Tungi rejim" (night mode) row.** Night mode already switches on by itself from 21:00 to 06:00, and nobody has asked to turn it off. My pick: keep it, because a mum who finds the dark screen hard to read has no other way out. *Flagged only because it's the weakest row.*
