# Moon keepsake: where each screen comes from and why each part is there

Wireframes: the "4 Moon keepsake" row on [04 Wireframes](https://www.figma.com/design/bg1FIs5wZOY9m0wUWkTJTe/oyjon-app?node-id=3-5). References are the apps in [`../12-moon-keepsake-research.md`](../12-moon-keepsake-research.md).

None of the baby trackers turn a month into something to send. That gap is the moon. Logging fills it quietly, and each full moon becomes a card she shares with family on Telegram, which is how the app spreads.

## T1 Today, month-day card

**Based on:** Beli (a recap banner on the home feed starts it).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| Card on top of Today | She may have notifications off. The month-day still reaches her where she already looks. |
| Full moon with "4-oy toʻldi" | The moment in four words. |
| "Laylo bugun 4 oylik." | The fact families care about. |
| "Oyni ochish" | One tap to the moon. |
| Close × | Doesn't nag. The moon stays ready on M1. |

## M1 Current moon

**Based on:** Moonlitt (one moon on a plain sky, "Full in 13 days"), the Weather app ("Next Full Moon 2 days"). Stardust shows what to avoid: twenty moons at once.

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| One big moon filling up | The calm reward for logging. No streaks, only time. |
| "4-oy · 13-kun" | Which month of life and which day of it. |
| "3 oy 12 kun" | Her age in the words families use. |
| "Toʻlin oyga 19 kun" | Something gentle to look forward to. |
| "Oldingi oylar" row | Opens past cards again. Should, a light version of the Journey screen. |

## M1 Current moon, ready

**Based on:** Moonlitt's share card, held until she has time.

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| Full moon, "4-oy tayyor" | The card waits for her. Nothing expires. |
| "Laylo 4 oylik" | The milestone in plain words. |
| "Kartani ochish" | One tap to make the card. |

## M1 One sentence

**Based on:** Liven ("Suggest a question" above the box), stoic. (you can always skip the question).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| Oyim asks "4-oydan nimani eslab qolmoqchisiz?" | One line from her turns a card into a memory. |
| Text box with a 120 counter | Fits the card in every language, Cyrillic and Russian included. |
| Three starter chips | A blank box is hard for a tired or low-literacy mum. |
| "Oʻtkazib yuborish" | The card still looks finished without it. |

## M2 Keepsake card

**Based on:** Retro (big card, Save and Share under it, "Saved" toast), Instagram Close Friends (where the card ends up).

| Element | Why it's here |
|---|---|
| 9:16 card | Story size, the shape Telegram and Instagram stories use. |
| Moon, "4 oylik", name, dates, her sentence | A memory, not a report. No weights or feed counts. |
| Small "oyjon" at the bottom | Quiet credit. It's how relatives find the app. |
| "Ism va sanalarni yashirish" | For a public Instagram. Hiding the dates also hides the birth date. |
| Saqlash, Ulashish | Save to the gallery, or share through the phone's sheet, where Telegram already is. |
