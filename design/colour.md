# Brand colour

Final 12 Oct 2026. Figma: 05 Brand, board "Colour". These are brand colours. UI scales and tokens come later, in the design system.

Every indigo sits on hue 275 and every neutral on hue 88.

| Name | OKLCH | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|

| Night | 0.181 0.049 275 | #0C0F27 | Background after 21:00 |

| Cream | 0.919 0.050 88 | #F2E3BF | Text at night |

Night gradient (Figma style "Night gradient"): #262D5C, oklch(0.318 0.083 275), at the top to Night at the bottom.

Contrast (WCAG 2): Indigo ink on Paper 13.3:1, Cream on Night 14.8:1, Ink on Saffron 8.4:1, Ink 2 on Paper 7.7:1, Night saffron on Night 11.1:1, Ink on Turquoise 7.9:1. Saffron on Paper is 1.6:1, so it never carries text.

Changes from the first palette: Line, Muted, Night line and Saffron moved back onto their family hue; Night saffron lost 14% chroma; Muted got 0.002 more lightness. Everything else is unchanged.
