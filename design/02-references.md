# Step 2: References

Board: [02 Research and moodboard](https://www.figma.com/design/bg1FIs5wZOY9m0wUWkTJTe/oyjon-app?node-id=4-2) in Figma.

## Main reference
**[gaiababy.app](https://gaiababy.app/)**: a calm baby tracker that's close to oyjon in spirit.
- Tone: short, plain, reassuring lines written by parents ("No streaks. No daily goal.", "Made by two tired parents").
- Features close to ours: quick logs, "last feed" on home, nap ranges, shared caregiver timeline with who logged it, notifications off by default, WHO growth charts.
- Theme colour on the site is `#F6F7F4`, a pale green-grey off-white. I couldn't read the rest of its CSS from here, so fonts and the full palette still need checking.

## What Shokhnur liked from the first 10 (Oct 12)
| App | Keep | Drop |
|---|---|---|
| **Headspace** | Colours, overall vibe | Its fonts |
| **Lifesum** | Colours, vibe | Fonts that are too light. Something else feels off (to pin down) |
| **Airbnb** | Clean design | Too neutral for this theme |
| **Stoic** | How the font is used, the font itself | The neutral grey. Take its raw colours and shift them warmer in OKLCH |

Not picked: Stardust, Oura, Calm, Finch, Flo, How We Feel.

## What that points to so far
- Warm, friendly colour like Headspace and Lifesum, with Airbnb's clean layout.
- Type with real weight, not thin. Stoic's type is the favourite so far.
- Colour approach: start from neutral bases and warm them up in OKLCH (keep lightness and chroma, shift hue toward warm).
