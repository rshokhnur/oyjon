# 08 Onboarding research

How 11 apps on Mobbin take a new user from first open to first value, and what oyjon can borrow. This is the text version of the "Onboarding research" section on the [oyjon-flow FigJam board](https://www.figma.com/board/ZyESbImvUcKhpd37KaCcBN/oyjon-flow). The screenshots live on the board only.

Researched 12 Oct 2026. Gaia Baby, Huckleberry and Nara Baby aren't on Mobbin, so their notes come from public sources instead of screenshots.

## 1. The flows

### Reference apps not on Mobbin

**Gaia Baby.** Main reference. Notes from [gaiababy.app](https://gaiababy.app/).

- Free, with no subscription and no trial. The site says setup takes about a minute.
- Each caregiver signs in with their own account and everyone shares one timeline.
- Notifications are all off until you turn them on.
- Home is the baby's card, a "next nap likely" range and quick log buttons. "No streaks. No daily goal."
- I couldn't find its first-run screens. Worth installing and recording the flow before we lock ours.

**Huckleberry.** Notes from a [screensdesign.com recording](https://screensdesign.com/apps/huckleberry-baby-child/).

- About 16 steps. A feature carousel, then an account before any questions.
- Then birthday and premature birth, number of naps, the day's sleep times, the last sleep, goals and the child's name.
- A "building your plan" screen, a paywall with a 14-day trial, a notification pre-ask, then the system prompt.
- Heavy on sleep data up front, because its nap prediction needs it.

**Nara Baby.** Notes from the [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1444639029).

- Free and ad-free. Inviting caregivers is a main feature, and timers show up for the whole family as Live Activities.
- Also tracks the mum's own health after birth.
- I found no record of its onboarding steps. Needs a fresh install to capture.

### Flows on Mobbin

**[Finch](https://mobbin.com/flows/ab3823c2-2068-4b25-965a-c4a2f9290102).** 8 screens, pet setup, no paywall here.

- The bird hatches first and you name it. You care for something before you type a word about yourself.
- A Shuffle button suggests names, so nobody gets stuck.
- The bird asks your name in a speech bubble. Questions come from the character, not from a form.
- No account, permission or paywall in this part.

**[Headspace](https://mobbin.com/flows/7cdc08c0-3bcb-4882-90dd-5cf92019616f).** 18 screens, account first, paywall at the end.

- Opens with a breathing animation, so the first seconds are already the product.
- Then the heaviest sign-up in the set. A terms checkbox, a full form and password rules before any value.
- Three benefit slides, one goal question, then you pick a first practice.
- Ends on a 14-day trial with a timeline of today, day 12 and day 14. Clear about when you pay.

**[Flo](https://mobbin.com/flows/d64dd348-5de3-40d0-8e2a-1fa781dad065).** 12 screens, consent before questions, no paywall shown.

- "Hello, I'm Flo." The brand talks in first person from screen 2.
- Health data consent gets its own screen, "Your body. Your data", with two ticks.
- Asks your name, then gives a whole screen to "Nice to meet you, jane!"
- Who it's for, year of birth, then "Are you pregnant?" with three big buttons. The mode choice comes last.

**[Lifesum](https://mobbin.com/flows/f373ce15-3f4e-4d88-aa24-365969ad35eb).** 17 screens, progress bar, account at the end.

- Two slides of social proof, App of the Day and 65 million users.
- One question per screen with a thin progress bar and a line saying why it asks.
- After your weight, a toast says "Great, we've got your starting point." Small rewards along the way.
- A counter runs to 100%, then "Your personalized plan is ready" and only then sign-up.

**[Calm](https://mobbin.com/flows/38549b00-2fe8-4fd1-914b-13c4905db245).** 10 screens, paywall at screen 4, account optional.

- Opens on "take a deep breath" over a gradient. The same move as Headspace.
- One multi-select goal question, then straight to a paywall with three plans and a renewal reminder toggle.
- "How did you hear about Calm?" comes after the paywall.
- The account is last and optional. You can reach home without one.

**[Stoic](https://mobbin.com/flows/0c54c507-3659-4b57-a758-adaa44ec3aeb).** 13 screens, skip on every step, paywall at the end.

- "meet stoic. your mental health companion", with a big bird drawing.
- Each question says "Your selections won't limit access to any features." Answering feels safe.
- You set your own reminder times next to a sample notification, and only then see the system prompt. The best notification ask here.
- "all set up, your plan is ready" as a checklist, then a 3-day trial.

**[How We Feel](https://mobbin.com/flows/95dc221a-5b99-4686-99a7-2a638cce8326).** 15 screens, first log inside onboarding, free.

- Cinematic intro with real people and video. Slow and confident.
- Says "It's free, made possible by donations" and "This app isn't the same thing as therapy" before asking anything.
- A 2/9 step counter, one "why are you here" question, then "Great! How We Feel will help you" plays it back.
- Your first check-in happens inside onboarding. You log a feeling before you see home.

**[Oura](https://mobbin.com/flows/3e8ee58d-6206-4422-971e-1a82f3710e73).** 23 screens, health permission mid-flow, the longest.

- A passkey offer, country, birth date, height, weight and sex, each with a "Why we ask" chip.
- Apple Health and a research opt-in get one screen each, with a quiet "No thanks".
- Focus area, a sleep self-rating and activities, then fitting the ring.
- A good model for consent screens. Far too long for a tired parent.

**[Stardust](https://mobbin.com/flows/a1d8818f-0b5d-4119-a937-6198c565c15b).** 12 screens, Sign in with Apple, moon phases.

- Moon and stars from the splash, with "Sync your cycle with the moon". Close to our story.
- Sign in with Apple sits in a bottom sheet over the art. Two taps.
- "Greetings earthling! Choose your journey" with four illustrated cards.
- The due date picker shows the baby's star sign inside a ring of moon phases. A date turns into a keepsake on the spot.

**[Peanut](https://mobbin.com/flows/420b8821-bcff-4357-8910-fcd18f5d6265).** 10 screens, extra, child profile.

- Asks your life stage (trying, mama-to-be, mama, menopause) to match you with women like you.
- The child form has an optional name, birth date or age, and gender with "Prefer not to say".
- Interest packs, then a selfie check. A community app, so trust comes first.

**[Duolingo](https://mobbin.com/flows/4511e153-c7d5-4b70-b37e-09cc798e49d9).** 16 screens, extra, value before account.

- This flow starts after the first lesson. Duo says "Don't lose your progress! Let's create a profile."
- You sign up to keep something you already made, which is a strong reason.
- One field per screen under a green progress bar, a contacts pre-ask, then Duo says "Welcome, Sam Lee!"

### Patterns

- **Length.** Flows run 8 to 23 screens, most of them 12 to 17. A mum at 3am won't sit through 17. Aim to reach Today in 6 or 7.
- **Account timing** splits three ways. First (Headspace, Huckleberry, Stardust), after the answers (Lifesum, Duolingo), or optional (Calm). Asking last gives her a reason to sign up, because there's something to save.
- **Personalisation.** Almost every app asks one "why are you here" question and plays it back as "your plan is ready". The loading bars are theatre. An honest playback works without them.
- **Good flows explain each question.** Oura has a "Why we ask" chip, Lifesum a one-line reason, Stoic says your answers won't limit access.
- **Permissions.** Nobody asks for notifications on screen 1. Stoic lets you pick times next to a sample notification first. Huckleberry pre-asks. Gaia leaves them all off.
- **Paywall.** Paid apps put it at the end, right after "your plan is ready". Calm puts it at screen 4. Free apps say out loud that they're free.
- **First value.** The best ones give it early. Breathing on screen 1 (Calm, Headspace), a first check-in (How We Feel), a finished lesson (Duolingo), a due date turned into a star sign (Stardust).
- **Companion.** It works when it does something. Finch's bird hatches and asks your name. Duo guards your progress. The Stoic and Headspace characters only decorate.
- **Progress.** A thin bar (Lifesum, Duolingo), a step count (How We Feel) or dots on slides. Most question screens have Skip.

## 3. Ideas for oyjon

For young mums in Uzbekistan, with Oyim as the companion, four languages, and everything working offline first. Trimmed to v1, so baby only and one mum per account. Ask with AI is in v1. Pregnancy, family sharing, caregivers, voice, milestones, the moon journey and Plus come later.

1. Language first, with nothing to read. Four pills, each written in its own script, with the phone's language preselected. Oyim rises like the moon.
2. Oyim asks the questions in her own voice, next to her and never in a bubble over content. Like Finch, but quieter.
3. Ask only what Today needs, which is the baby's name and birth date. Weight, photo and sex can wait.
4. Sign in after the first moment. Everything saves on the phone first. Then "Back it up so nothing is lost" with a phone code, or "Later".
5. First value is her moon. Right after the birth date, Moon 4 fills up to today and shows "3 oy 12 kun". Our version of Stardust's star sign.
6. One optional first log. "When was the last feed?" with time chips. Today then opens on a real "time since" card instead of an empty state.
7. One honest consent screen. Flo's "your body, your data" plus How We Feel's "this isn't a doctor". Health consent is required, the question log is optional, each with its own toggle.
8. Ask for notifications later, the Stoic way. Show a sample ("Moon 4 is full") and let her choose. Everything off by default, like Gaia.
9. No paywall. v1 is free, so say it plainly, the way How We Feel does.
10. Show progress as five small moons that fill up, instead of a bar.
11. Opened between 21:00 and 06:00? Onboarding runs in the night palette, slower, with a sleepy Oyim.

### A possible flow

Seven steps from first open to Today, with the moon at step 3.

1. Language, Oyim rises
2. Baby's name and birth date
3. The moon appears and fills to today (first value)
4. Consent, one screen
5. Last feed? (optional)
6. Back it up with a phone code, or Later
7. Today

Notifications come later, from Today.

## 4. Decisions

Decided on 12 Oct 2026:

1. She signs in after the moon appears. Everything stays on the phone until then, and "Later" skips sign-in.
2. A first feed log is one optional step in onboarding.
3. Oyim asks every question in her own voice. The AI companion is the heart of the app.

The locked v1 onboarding is O1 Welcome and language, O2 Baby profile, O3 First moment (the moon), O4 Consent, O5 First feed (optional), O6 Sign in (or Later), then Today.

Also decided on 12 Oct 2026:

4. Data stays on the phone before consent and sign-in. A lawyer checks it against Uzbekistan's personal data law before launch.
5. Offline, she taps "Later" at sign-in and Today reminds her once when she's back online.
6. Ask with AI is in v1 with 3 questions a week and no paywall. After that it says "back on Monday", and the cards stay open.

To do before the final screens:

1. Record Gaia, Huckleberry and Nara on a real phone, since Mobbin doesn't have them.
2. Test the flow with 3 to 5 mums.

## 5. UX review

Done on 13 Oct 2026. I walked the locked flow step by step as a tired mum at night, one hand free, on a cheap Android, sometimes offline, in any of the four languages. The steps stay as locked. The fixes are about what happens around them. The diagram on the [Onboarding page](https://www.figma.com/board/ZyESbImvUcKhpd37KaCcBN/oyjon-flow?node-id=12-835) now shows each one as a numbered badge.

About 10 taps and one name to type from first open to Today.

### Revised flow

1. O1 Welcome and language. The phone's language is picked already. A small "I already use oyjon" link goes to O6 and restores the diary (question 1).
2. O2 Baby profile. The name is optional and "Chaqaloq" stands in. The birth date offers Today and Yesterday chips, then a calendar.
3. O3 First moment. The moon fills to today. Born today shows "Moon 1 starts today".
4. O4 Consent. One required yes and one optional switch. Saying no explains why and keeps nothing.
5. O5 First feed, optional. Earlier, "Hozir" (feeding now) which starts the timer, or skip.
6. O6 Back it up, or Later. Offline and late SMS fall back to Later.
7. T1 Today. The next day, one card offers the moon notification.

Every step saves on the phone, so a closed app reopens on the same step.

### Issues and fixes

| # | Priority | What goes wrong | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | must | A new phone or a reinstall has no way back in, so she types the baby again and gets two diaries | "I already use oyjon" link on O1 goes to sign-in and restores the diary (question 1) |
| 2 | must | Many babies get their name at a family gathering days after the birth, and a required name blocks step 2 | Name is optional, "Chaqaloq" stands in, Settings changes it later |
| 3 | must | A date wheel is slow one-handed and easy to get wrong | Today and Yesterday chips, then a calendar. A future date gets a gentle no. Older than a year still works |
| 4 | should | Born today shows an empty moon | Oyim says "Moon 1 starts today" and the first sliver lights up |
| 5 | must | Switching off the required health consent had no next step | One line on why the diary needs it. Nothing she typed is kept, and she can change her mind on the same screen |
| 6 | must | Many mums install the app mid-feed, and "When was the last feed?" doesn't fit | A "Hozir" chip starts the feed timer and Today opens with it running |
| 7 | must | SMS is late or never comes, the internet drops | Later always works, resend after 60 seconds, Today reminds her once online |
| 8 | must | Signing in with a number that already has a backup could wipe one diary | Keep the saved diary and add what she logged today, with no choice screen |
| 9 | must | "Ask for notifications later" had no time or place | One card on Today the day after onboarding, with a sample. Off until she says yes |
| 10 | must | The baby wakes and the app is closed half-way | Each step saves on the phone, she lands back on the same step |
| 11 | should | Night opening | 21:00 to 06:00 uses the night palette, a sleepy Oyim and no sound |
| 12 | should | Copy that assumes "mum" leaves out dads and grandmothers | Copy talks about the baby and says "siz". Consent stays three short lines |

### Keep or drop, decided 13 Oct 2026

1. An "I already use oyjon" link on O1 that signs in and restores the diary. It reuses O6, so there's no new screen. Decided: keep, must.

## 6. Global-ready

Added on 13 Oct 2026. Uzbekistan is the default, and nothing here is fixed to it. The full list is in [`14-global-ready.md`](14-global-ready.md).

- O1 picks the language from the phone and the country from the SIM. One small line shows the country, "Oʻzbekiston · oʻzgartirish", and opens the country picker (the one proposal in [design/14](14-global-ready.md)).
- The language list starts with the country's usual order. Uzbekistan shows Lotin, Кирилл, Русский, English.
- O2's calendar starts the week on the country's first day, Monday in Uzbekistan. Dates use her language's format.
- O3's age line uses her language's plural rules, so "3 oy 12 kun" and "3 месяца 12 дней" both fit.
- O4's consent lines come from the country's legal pack. Uzbekistan uses the personal data law text the lawyer checks.
- O5's time chips show 24-hour or 12-hour time, whichever the phone uses.
- O6's phone code comes from her country, +998 for Uzbekistan, and opens the same picker. Where SMS doesn't reach, only Apple and Google show.
