# UX principles

1. **One hand, two taps.** Core actions (start a feed, log a diaper) reachable with a thumb in two taps.
2. **Night-friendly.** Every screen works in dark mode; no bright flashes.
3. **Calm language.** Explain, don't alarm. Red flags are clear but not scary.
4. **Big targets.** Minimum 48dp tap targets, readable text at default size.
5. **Offline first.** Nothing breaks without internet.
6. **Local first.** Uzbek Latin copy is written first, not translated last.
