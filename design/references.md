# Visual references

Screenshots from other apps and sites are reference material, not ours to publish, so they stay out of this public repo. They live in the local `design/` folder on Shokhnur's Mac. Copy them over by hand to work from another computer.

| Local folder | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `inspiration-full/` | 40 full-resolution app screens (Stardust, Oura, Calm, Finch, Headspace, Flo, How We Feel, Lifesum, Airbnb, Stoic) | Mobbin |
| `inspiration-previews/` | The same 40 screens at preview size | Mobbin |
| `icon-refs/` | 73 app icons plus 8 close-ups, for the app icon style | appicons.store (Dmitri Litvinov) |
| `oyim-refs/` | Frames from Benji Taylor's Grok Bot animation clip | x.com/benjitaylor |

The Figma moodboard (page "02 Research and moodboard") already holds the Mobbin screens, so most people won't need the local copies.
